What the system does

Built around the way a herd actually gets managed

Identify

AI breed identification

A model trained on native cattle and buffalo breeds reads a photo or live camera feed and returns the breed with a confidence score, ready to attach to a record.

Register

Digital farmer portal

Farmers register animals against a tag ID, review breed history at a glance, and keep every record for the herd in one searchable place.

Treat

Veterinary care & history

Authorised doctors log medical visits, track vaccination schedules, and leave health notes tied to the same animal record the farmer sees.
